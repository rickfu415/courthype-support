COURTHYPE SUPPORT

Turn any court into an arena.

No sound, or too quiet?

Turn the phone's volume up and connect a Bluetooth speaker from the start page. CourtHype plays even when the phone is in silent mode.

Bought CourtHype Pro on another phone?

Open the Pro screen and tap "Restore purchase". You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account.

What does the free trial include?

Every day you can use Courtside mode and the Playoffs pace free for 5 minutes. CourtHype Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks both for good.
