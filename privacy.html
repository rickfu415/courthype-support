COURTHYPE PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 22, 2026

CourtHype does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not use the microphone, camera, contacts or location.
  • Your settings (team color, mixer levels) are stored only on your device.
  • The optional in-app purchase is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that purchase.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
